What is this?

Semonides of Amorgos was a Greek poet writing in the 7th century BC. In a surviving poem generally known as the Types of Women, he describes ten kinds of women by comparing them with animals or forces of nature.

There are pig-women who supposedly prefer eating to cleaning, fox-women who notice too much, donkey-women who are sexually promiscuous, dog-women who are disobedient, stormy sea-women who are changeable, Earth-women who are greedy, weasel-women who steal, horse-women who are lazy and vain, ape-women who are unattractive and bee-women who work hard.

The bee is the only category he approves of.

The premise of this app: treat Semonides' taxonomy with exactly the amount of scientific respect it deserves. Answer ten questions and the app will decide which of his ten stereotypes you are, then explain what the classification actually says about his worldview.
  • The categories are Semonides' stereotypes, not descriptions of women.
  • The app is for women because that is the population Semonides chose to classify.
  • After your result, you can inspect all ten categories.
